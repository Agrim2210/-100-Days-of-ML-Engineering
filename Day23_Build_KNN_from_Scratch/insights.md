# Insights — KNN

## Observations

1. KNN performed well on small dataset
2. No training time — all computation at prediction
3. Scaling significantly improved performance

---



## Advantages

- Simple and intuitive
- No training required
- Works well for small datasets

---

## Disadvantages

- Slow for large datasets
- Sensitive to noise
- Curse of dimensionality

---

## Key Learning

KNN is not about learning parameters,
it is about **memorizing data + smart distance calculation**
